About Me

I am a Scientist in the Division of Agricultural Engineering at ICAR–Indian Agricultural Research Institute, New Delhi. My work focuses on engineering solutions and data-driven approaches for efficient irrigation, sustainable water resources management and climate-resilient agriculture.

My research integrates agricultural water management with geospatial technologies, remote sensing, geographic information systems and artificial intelligence to support evidence-based planning and decision-making.

Geospatial & AI Applications

Decision-support approaches for groundwater resource assessment, management and sustainable land and water use.
